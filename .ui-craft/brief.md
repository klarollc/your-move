# Your Move design brief

Status: Historical office-first product direction; hosted single-user Your Move product now governs the active surface.

## Product identity

Your Move is a work-management product for directing real multi-agent workflows. The current hosted surface is a plain-language work interface with provider connections, execution progress, owner review, and explicit proposal approval. The older office-scene thesis and its art-direction requirements remain historical context for the graybox office surface, not the active hosted interface.

## Design principles

- Truth before theater: meaningful visual behavior must correspond to inspectable workflow state.
- Management language first: use outcomes, assignments, questions, approvals, evidence, and delivery before framework terminology.
- Progressive disclosure: show the work first; reveal technical evidence and settings on demand.
- Calm attention: distinguish owner actions from agent activity and background status.
- Accessible equivalence: preserve keyboard operation, readable labels, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Preserve product safety and consent boundaries: no irreversible write without the appropriate explicit approval.

Historical references to an office scene, spatial metaphor, and office-first hierarchy describe the earlier product thesis. They are not authority to undo the hosted Your Move work surface or its owner-approved R1–R4 behavior.
