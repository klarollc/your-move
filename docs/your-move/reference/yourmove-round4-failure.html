Your Move — Round 4: Failure States

Where the truthfulness rules earn their keep. Four failures, each rendered as a fact about the world — never as a spinner, a guess, or a silently retried hope.

A Runtime disconnect — the projection goes stale

The event feed drops mid-session. The screen says exactly how old its truth is, and a ruling made while offline is held in an outbox — the matter stays lit until the log actually appends.

  • A1The banner ages the truth. "Projection of r512 — 4 minutes old" is the staleness contract made visible. No spinner pretending the world is loading; the world is simply not arriving.
  • A2The lamp doesn't go out on intent. Your ruling sits in the outbox and the matter stays lit — dashed border, "not recorded yet." The glow extinguishes when the log appends, not when you click. This is rule 5 (no optimistic UI) applied to the outage.
  • A3Outbox is counted, not hidden. "Outbox: 1" in the topbar — held rulings are first-class facts, visible from every view.

D Damaged log — the log disagrees with the view

Rule 7 in action: the projection and the log diverge, and a stretch of events is unreadable. The UI announces the disagreement and renders the gap as a gap — never interpolated.
