Your Move — Round 6: Character

Three ideas that give the app a face without touching its honesty: a master lamp with a heartbeat, time travel on the log, and the two-sided journey — every governance act as a visible trip, worker → you → worker.

1 The master lamp

One lamp in the topbar, visible from every view — including the calm screen and the log. Lit means something, somewhere, is your move. It is the app's heartbeat, not a notification badge.

  • L1One lamp to rule the glow. Every per-row lamp in the app is a reflection of this one. It answers "is anything my move?" without scrolling, from any view — the calm screen included, where it sits unlit above the big lamp.
  • L2Clicking it takes you to your move. The lamp is a control, not a decoration: one click → Today view, your-move group expanded. The count is mono, quiet, exact.

  • T1The whole screen time-travels, not just the log. List, rail, calm screen, as-of — everything re-folds at r505. A log-only scrubber would be a museum exhibit; this is the product's memory made navigable.
  • T2The past is read-only, loudly. Signal-colored banner (not terra — this isn't an error), "Return to present" as the primary action, and every ruling control disabled with the reason: "You can't rule on the past."
  • T3The spine shows where you are. "◀ you are here" at r505; later events dim but stay listed — the future isn't hidden, it's marked as not-yet.
  • T4Answers real questions. "What did my Tuesday look like?" "When exactly did this become my move?" — the audit trail becomes an experience, not just a compliance feature.

  • J1Two-sided, as you asked. Arrival (worker → you) and departure (you → worker) are the same component run in opposite directions. Governance reads as a round trip, not a notification.
  • J2The ghost, not the card. The real cards never move — the projection re-folds instantly and a ghost flies the old→new path. The UI can't be caught mid-lie with a card in two places.
  • J3Departures are tinted by fate. Accepted glows moss on its way to Done; sent-back travels plain back to In progress; a deferral drifts to Deferred. The journey narrates the ruling.
  • J4The lamp keeps time. It lights as the arrival lands and dims as the departure settles — the master lamp and the journeys are one system.
