## Visual thesis

A grounded contemporary studio with subtle miniature-like warmth. The office should feel inhabited, calm, capable, and legible—not whimsical, toy-like, photorealistic, or dashboard-shaped.

The scene uses a fixed three-quarter isometric view, light wood, off-white plaster, charcoal metal, muted textiles, restrained greenery, soft daylight, and warm practical lamps. Amber is reserved for manager attention, focus, and action.

## Originality and provenance

- Produce original CuckCode compositions and system rules.
- Do not imitate a named living artist or reproduce a commercial product's distinctive visual identity.
- References may establish general architectural, material, ergonomic, photographic, or historical qualities.
- Record source URL/file, creator where known, license/usage basis, retrieval date, and the specific abstract quality referenced.
- Generated or commissioned assets remain Candidate until owner approval and manifest promotion.

## Controlled comparison frame

All three candidates must show the same canonical default-office fixture:

- viewport: 1440×900;
- documented 1280×800 crop/reflow plan;
- fixed camera and office footprint;
- six distinct admitted agents at their role work areas;
- manager desk, team area, collaboration table, Project Library, review station, delivery area, and bounded reception;
- no escalations, one current priority, connected runtime health, inspector closed, attention rail collapsed;
- identical labels, content density, and state data;
- no decorative variation that changes information hierarchy.
- visible collaboration objects have stable machine-readable counterparts and do not imply activity absent canonical events;
- agent attention, uncertainty, requests, and feedback are legible without reducing agents to decorative status indicators.

## Candidate differentiation

All candidates remain within the approved thesis. They should explore controlled emphasis, not different products.

### Candidate A — Architectural clarity

Emphasize strong floor-plane zoning, clean silhouettes, restrained props, and immediate role/location comprehension. Risk: becoming sterile or diagrammatic.

### Candidate B — Human studio warmth

Emphasize muted textiles, individual desk objects, soft practical lighting, and agent-authored identity details. Risk: visual noise or false anthropomorphic sentiment.

### Candidate C — Miniature craft precision

Emphasize material edges, contained depth, contact shadows, carefully staged objects, and model-like spatial coherence. Risk: reading as a toy, diorama, or game instead of a serious work environment.

The Product Designer may rename these candidates but must preserve the three comparative emphases unless the owner approves a change.

## Required candidate board

Each candidate delivers:

1. full default-office composition;
2. 1280×800 accommodation diagram;
3. semantic palette and contrast intent;
4. wood, plaster, metal, textile, greenery, glass, and paper material samples;
5. typography hierarchy with humanist management text and restrained monospace technical text;
6. avatar silhouette/scale sheet showing inclusive variation and role-neutral personal expression;
7. two approved-system personalization examples per agent: accessory and desk object;
8. lighting and contact-shadow callouts;
9. prop-density and negative-space rules;
10. amber attention/focus examples;
11. motion character and reduced-motion translation;
12. strengths, risks, exclusions, implementation complexity, and asset needs.

## Avatar contract

- simplified adult proportions; neither chibi nor photorealistic;
- distinguish roles through workspace, tools, context, label, and behavior—not demographic stereotypes;
- support varied skin tones, hair, body presentation, gender expression, and mobility aids;
- keep facial detail restrained at canonical scale;
- allow each agent to author display name, pronouns/form of address, appearance direction, accessory, desk object, rationale, and one novel Candidate detail;
- novel details require safety, accessibility, art-system, asset, and manager review before Active use;
- personal expression cannot communicate false workflow state or imply verified consciousness.

## Golden-state production sequence

After owner selection or coherent reconciliation, produce:

1. default office at both viewports;
2. active collaboration tied to a canonical collaboration event;
3. manager escalation with a single clear attention path;
4. blocked work with reason available and no fake activity;
5. visual-review state with named artifact/evidence;
6. reduced-motion equivalents preserving all meaning.

Each state includes its fixture/event sequence, scene descriptor, DOM-outline expectation, viewport/environment manifest, visible-state checklist, accessibility notes, and owner approval record.

## Review rubric

Score each candidate from 1–5 with written evidence:

- office-before-dashboard clarity;
- six-role glance readability;
- manager attention legibility;
- calmness and restraint;
- warmth without toy-like character;
- material coherence and depth;
- inclusive agent expression;
- accessible contrast/focus intent;
- feasibility in PixiJS/WebGL and DOM shell;
- behavior under 1280×800 and reduced motion;
- originality and provenance quality;
- reciprocal legibility: human observers understand agent needs and contributions without the scene obscuring agent-facing state;
- collaboration clarity without fake proximity, activity, or consensus;
- visual accommodation of agent-authored identity without weakening role, state, or accessibility semantics.

Scores support discussion; they do not determine selection automatically.

## Approval gate

The owner may select A, B, or C; reject all; or request a coherent reconciliation naming the retained traits and base candidate. No high-fidelity asset, token, or screenshot baseline becomes Active before explicit owner approval.
