## Required use

Before proposing or changing UI, read the local pack and name the governing AR plate. Keep the office the default primary surface, use plain-language cues, reveal one contextual interaction at a time, and expose technical evidence through deliberate drill-down. Distinguish a dependency blocker, a manager question, a review request and unavailable information.

Reject persistent dashboard card grids, metric panels, dense technical sidebars, stacked contextual surfaces, and office-as-thumbnail layouts. A deliberately opened evidence view may use the full surface and must provide a clear return.

Use the qualifications in the constitution: names and work are fictional; imagery is conceptual; document framing is not a product token; raster colors are not exact token values. Canonical truth and operability still control. If a real conflict arises, obtain an explicit design amendment before changing behavior.

Future visual reviews must show current-build comparisons to named plates at the existing approved viewports, with the required interaction, keyboard, reduced-motion and console/network checks. Concept approval is not application visual acceptance.

If the local pack is missing or any approved hash differs, stop the affected visual work and obtain the exact approved artifacts or an owner-approved successor. Do not regenerate an approximation and treat it as approved. This local-only distribution limitation remains intentional; no commit, upload or publication of the artwork is authorized here.
